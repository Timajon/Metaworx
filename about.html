Panel beater · Mbombela

A Lowveld workshop on a national repair standard.

Metaworx Autobody is the Auto Magic agent for Mbombela. The people, the booth, and the gate are here in Riverside Park.

Drivers looking up the shop will also find it as Meta Worx Lowveld, Meta Worx Autobody Lowveld, and DMR Nelspruit. Same gate: 4 Emnotweni Avenue, Riverside Park.

DMR Nelspruit belongs to Metaworx. Diamond magrim repair is booked here, with the panel and paint work, not through a separate workshop.

The workshop repairs passenger cars and light bakkies. Collision damage, paint, dents, hail, and diamond magrim repair come through one assessment, then a repair that is scoped before it starts.

Auto Magic is the panel-beating network. Metaworx is the local workshop. Questions about a car in our care come to the shop first, on 013 101 3183 or tim@metaworx.co.za.

At drop-off

What to bring with the car.

If the vehicle cannot be driven, phone first. We will tell you whether it should come in on a flatbed, and which gate to use.

In the car

  • The vehicle, the keys, and a number we can reach during the repair.
  • Your name as it should appear on the job.
  • Photos only help the first call. The assessment is done on the car.

If you are claiming

  • Insurer name and claim number.
  • Assessor details, if someone has already been appointed.
  • The repair waits until that authorisation is confirmed.

How a job moves

Nothing starts on a handshake in the parking bay.

  1. Check-in

    The car is logged, the visible damage is walked, and hidden damage is flagged.

  2. Scope

    Parts, repair, and paint are listed. Insured work stays on hold for approval.

  3. In the booth

    Panel work, preparation, and paint happen in that order, not all at once.

  4. Handover

    You are called when the car has been checked and is ready to leave.
