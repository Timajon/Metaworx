Diamond magrim repair · Nelspruit

DMR Nelspruit belongs to Metaworx.

Diamond-cut mag rims and kerbed alloy wheels are repaired at the Metaworx workshop on Cascades Close. One gate in Riverside Park, Mbombela. Not a separate shop.

If you have been looking for DMR Nelspruit, this is the workshop. The diamond face is refinished here, after the rim has been checked.

A kerb scrape, a scuffed lip, or a dull diamond-cut face comes in with the car. The wheel is looked at for cracks and buckles before any refinishing starts. A rim that is still sound is repaired so it sits with the other wheels. A rim that is cracked or badly buckled is called out before work starts.

Panel beating and spray painting stay on the same job card when the body needs it too. Book DMR Nelspruit on 013 757 0354 or tim@metaworx.co.za.

01

Kerb rash

The outer lip takes the hit against a pavement or a driveway. The rash is repaired when the rim is still round and sound.

  • Scuffed outer lips
  • Front and rear alloy rims
  • Checked before the face is touched

02

Diamond-cut faces

The machined diamond face dulls, marks, or loses its edge. DMR Nelspruit refinishes that face so the wheel matches the set.

  • Diamond-cut mag rims
  • Face refinishing after the repair
  • Same workshop as the panel shop
