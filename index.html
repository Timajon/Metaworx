What comes into the shop

Repairs with a finish you can stand next to.

A scrape in a parking bay and a quarter panel after a bump follow the same rule: look first, then repair, then paint so the panel belongs on the car again.

01 Collision and panel beating Panels pulled, replaced, or reshaped, then lined up before they see primer.
02 Spray painting Colour mixed to the car, including fade on older paint. 03 Hail and dents Bonnets, roofs, and boot lids after a Lowveld storm. 04 DMR Nelspruit Diamond magrim repair. It belongs to Metaworx.

All workshop services

From the gate to collection

A repair with the sequence written down.

You hear the scope before the tools come out. If an insurer is paying, the booking waits on their authorisation.

  1. Call it in

    Phone the workshop or send the damage, the vehicle, and a claim number if you have one.

  2. Assess

    The car is checked on Cascades Close. Extra damage is noted before parts are ordered.

  3. Repair and paint

    Metal, plastic, and paint move through the booth. Colour is matched to the panel beside it.

  4. Collect

    After a final look, we call you. Bring the keys arrangement you made at drop-off.
