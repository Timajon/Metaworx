Straight answers

Do you repair cars that are not on an insurance claim?

Yes. Self-funded repairs are assessed the same way. Say so on the enquiry and leave the insurer field blank.

How long will the repair take?

It depends on parts and how much of the car is damaged. The workshop gives a timeframe after the assessment, not from a photo.

Do you repair mag rims?

Yes. DMR Nelspruit belongs to Metaworx, so diamond magrim repair is done at this workshop. That covers kerbed and scuffed alloy rims, including diamond-cut faces. The wheel is checked before the face is refinished.

Can I fix hail myself first?

Leave the dents. A home attempt makes a proper repair harder and can turn a paintless repair into a respray.

Where do I park when I arrive?

2 Cascades Close, Riverside Park, behind Volvo. If you are unsure at the industrial entrance, call 013 757 0354 from the gate.
