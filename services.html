Workshop services

Metal, paint, and the parts between them.

Passenger cars and light bakkies. Every job starts with an assessment at 2 Cascades Close so the repair matches the damage, not a guess from a photo alone.

01

Collision and panel beating

Bumps that crease a door, a fender, or a rear quarter. Panels are repaired where the metal will hold a straight finish, and replaced where it will not. Gaps and shut lines are set before paint.

  • Doors, guards, bonnets, and boot lids
  • Low-speed impact and car-park knocks
  • Strip, repair, and refit

02

Spray painting and colour match

A single panel or a larger area. Paint is mixed to the vehicle colour code and adjusted when the existing finish has faded in the Lowveld sun, so the new paint does not sit as a hard-edged patch.

  • Panel resprays
  • Blend into the neighbouring panel
  • Bumper and plastic refinishing

03

Scratches, scuffs, and dents

Trolley marks, gate scrapes, and small dents that have not torn the paint through to the metal. If a panel can be repaired instead of replaced, that is the first option.

  • Parking-bay scrapes
  • Small dents with the paint still intact
  • Local refinishing

04

Hail damage

Storms across Mbombela mark bonnets, roofs, and boot lids in a pattern, not as one dent. We count the spread, then repair panels that will come back clean and replace those that will not.

  • Bonnet, roof, and boot lid
  • Assessment before any DIY attempt
  • Glass damage noted for the claim

05

Parts and plastic repair

Bumpers, grilles, brackets, and lamps. Repairable plastics are reshaped and painted. Parts that will not hold are replaced and fitted so the bumper line matches the body.

  • Front and rear bumpers
  • Clips, brackets, and grilles
  • Replacement when a repair will not last

06

Diamond magrim repair

Kerbed and scuffed mag rims, including diamond-cut faces. The wheel is checked for cracks and buckles first. A rim that is still sound is repaired, and the diamond face is refinished so it sits with the other wheels.

  • Kerb rash and scuffed outer lips
  • Diamond-cut face refinishing
  • Cracked or badly buckled rims called out before work starts

07

Insurance assessments

If you are claiming, bring the insurer’s name and the claim number with the vehicle. The damage is recorded at the workshop and the repair is booked once authorisation is in place.

  • Check-in against the claim
  • Scope agreed before the repair starts
  • Self-funded work quoted the same way
